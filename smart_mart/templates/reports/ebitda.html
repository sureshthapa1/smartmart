    What is EBITDA?

    EBITDA = Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation & Amortisation
    It measures operating profitability before non-cash charges (depreciation) and financing costs (interest/tax). For retail businesses it's a cleaner measure of core business performance than net profit.

    Formula: EBITDA = Net Profit + D&A + Interest + Tax
    Healthy EBITDA margin: >15% for retail
    To record depreciation or interest expenses, go to Expenses and set the type to depreciation or interest.
